Biography

Hülya Uşan

N. Hülya Uşan is a Turkish artist, curator, educator, and cultural manager specializing in Traditional Turkish Arts. Her artistic practice encompasses Katı' (paper-cut art), Ebru (Turkish marbling), traditional bookbinding, watercolor, and oil painting. Through her work, she is committed to preserving, promoting, and transmitting Türkiye's rich artistic heritage to future generations.

She graduated from the Department of Handicrafts Education at Gazi University and completed both a Non-Thesis Master's Degree in Traditional Turkish Arts at Necmettin Erbakan University and a Thesis Master's Degree in Visual Communication Design at Ankara Yıldırım Beyazıt University.

After many years as a visual arts educator, Uşan founded the Çukurambar Culture and Art Center (ÇKSM) in 2021. As its founder and director, she leads educational programs, exhibitions, symposiums, workshops, and national and international cultural projects dedicated to preserving and promoting Traditional Turkish Arts.

She is also the Founding Chairwoman of the International Nakkaş Culture, Art and Literature Association, where she develops cultural initiatives and international collaborations focused on safeguarding cultural heritage.

Uşan currently serves as a Katı' Art Instructor under the General Directorate of Living Heritage and Cultural Activities, Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the Republic of Türkiye. Recognized as a Ministry of Culture Artist, she is widely known for her contributions to the preservation and international promotion of the art of Katı'.

Following the traditional master-apprentice lineage of Turkish arts, she received her Ebru Ijazah (Master Certification) from Fuad Başar, one of Türkiye's most distinguished calligraphers and marbling masters, officially recognized as a Living Human Treasure.

Throughout her career, Uşan has represented Türkiye at numerous international exhibitions, workshops, symposiums, and cultural programs in Iran, Pakistan, and China. She represented Türkiye at the International Holy Qur'an Exhibition in Tehran, presenting exhibitions, workshops, and lectures on the art of Katı'. She also participated in Turkish Airlines' Cultural Promotion Program in the People's Republic of China and conducted exhibitions and workshops in Pakistan upon the invitation of the Yunus Emre Institute.

As a curator, Uşan has led major social responsibility art projects in collaboration with the Ministry of Family and Social Services of the Republic of Türkiye, including "Art for Life" and "The Breath of the Homeland Through Art." These exhibitions, featuring artworks created by families of martyrs and veterans, have been presented at prestigious venues including the Presidential Complex of Türkiye, the Grand National Assembly of Türkiye, the National Palaces Museum Collections (Dolmabahçe Palace), ATO Congresium, and the Türkiye Culture Route Festivals.

Alongside her artistic production, she continues to create works in Katı', Ebru, watercolor, and oil painting while educating new generations of artists. She also coordinates the annual Ramadan Traditional Arts Workshops at Roketsan, bringing Traditional Turkish Arts to diverse audiences through corporate cultural initiatives.

Uşan has published academic work on the art of Katı' and has appeared on national television channels, including TRT and Diyanet TV, delivering lectures and interviews on Traditional Turkish Arts.

Believing that Traditional Turkish Arts constitute a living cultural language connecting history with the future, N. Hülya Uşan continues to contribute to cultural heritage through artistic production, education, curatorial practice, and international cultural collaborations, representing Turkish art on the global stage.
